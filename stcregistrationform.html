Our vision

Good students and good people.

Solomon Tuition Center began in May 2016 with a good ambition: to help every child learn well, grow in skills like Taekwondo, drama and dance, and become humble, disciplined and confident. Tuition here is free for all.

Expert teachers

Maths, Science, English and exam prep taught by teachers who explain the why, not just the answer.

Small batches

Fewer students per class means every doubt gets answered before the lesson moves on.

Weekly tests

A short test every week shows exactly what is strong and what needs another look.

Doubt clearing
